# Synthetic datasets

- `zabbix_trends_synthetic.csv`: imaginary Zabbix-style hourly metrics.
- `ocs_assets_synthetic.csv`: fictional OCS-style asset inventory.
- `sales_events.jsonl`: mock sales events with a duplicate ID.

They are **not exports** from Zabbix 7 or OCS Inventory 3. Their schemas are
illustrative and must not be presented as actual vendor database/API schemas.
Do not claim real LABFIK monitoring or inventory results from these files.
